Synthetic data. Every wallet, account and payment on this page was generated to test the matching logic. No real mobile-money or P2P records were used. Account IDs contain "SYN" and addresses start with "TSYN".

Cash-out leads

Wallet clusters that received funds traced from victim-reported wallets, linked to the mobile-money account that received shillings when they sold USDT to P2P traders. Each lead is for an analyst to verify through lawful process.

How links are made

Each USDT trade with a known P2P trader is matched to a payment on that trader's mobile-money statement. Confidence comes from repetition: one coincidence is never enough.

Stress test

The generator and the matcher share assumptions, so the baseline flatters the method. Each scenario is a fresh synthetic world that breaks one assumption.
